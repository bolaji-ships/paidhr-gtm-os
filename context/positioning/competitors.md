## Where PaidHR wins (evidence)
- Nigerian statutory depth: Innovation Village lists PaidHR "Nigerian-focused, supporting PAYE, pensions, NHF, and NSITF across multiple states", compliance "Very High", "Best for compliance-focused SMEs" (cmp Direct comparison).
- Remittance proof: customer cites receipts as evidence of payment (cv Video 2); PaidHR claims "zero defaults and zero delays" in 2025 (co §15a).
- Simplicity: Famasi CEO tried "a couple" of tools and chose Pade because it "looked very simple" (cv Video 1).
- Employee finance at no employer cost: EWA "Zero cost to your company" (co §3).
- Deskless access: WhatsApp and USSD launched in NG, GH, KE in Oct 2026 (co §15d). USSD not found for competitors (INFERRED from cmp).

## Where PaidHR loses (evidence)
- Scale and funding: SeamlessHR ~$25M and named enterprise logos vs PaidHR ~$2.3M to $2.97M (cmp §1; co §6).
- Multi-currency and multi-country: Innovation Village rates PaidHR multi-currency "Limited" (cmp Direct comparison); compliance expertise "centers on Nigeria" (cu §5).
- Price transparency and free offers: SoftSUITE publishes prices; Salario is free; Bujeti gives 2 free months (cmp §5, §7, §8). PaidHR publishes none (co §3).
- EOR: Deel, Remote, Oyster, Workpay sell EOR; PaidHR EOR Not found, partner RemoFirst instead (co §12).
- Review presence: no G2, Capterra or Trustpilot profiles found (cu §5).
