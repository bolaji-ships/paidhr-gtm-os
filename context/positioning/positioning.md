## Positioning statement

For growing African businesses, starting in Nigeria, that still run payroll and remittances on spreadsheets and disconnected tools, PaidHR is the full-suite HRMS that puts HR, payroll, statutory remittance and performance in one compliant platform. Unlike point tools and legacy systems, it gives every employee, desk-based or not, access to payslips, requests and earned wages through the app, WhatsApp or USSD, at no extra cost to the employer. PaidHR processed ₦119.2bn in payroll in 2026 to date and remitted ₦934M PAYE and ₦754M pension in 2025 with zero defaults (company claims; co §5, §15a).
