# PaidHR: Company Research (raw)

Checked: 2026-11-07
Method: web search + web fetch

Note on method: paidhr.com pages were read through WebFetch, which returns an AI-extracted summary of the page, not raw HTML. Quotes below are what that extraction returned as verbatim. Several third-party sites blocked fetching (TechCabal 403, LinkedIn 403, CB Insights 403, Dealroom 403, Apple App Store 404/429, Google Play returned an empty shell). ## 1. What they sell

- HR and payroll software ("people operations platform") for African businesses, plus embedded employee financial products (Earned Wage Access, employee wallet, loan marketplace, cross-border payroll). (Source: https://www.paidhr.com/, checked 2026-11-07)
- Homepage HTML title: "PaidHR: #1 People Operations Platform For HR Teams". (Source: https://www.paidhr.com/, checked 2026-11-07)
- H1: "One compliant HR Software for the entire employee lifecycle." (Source: https://www.paidhr.com/, checked 2026-11-07)
- Revenue model per press: subscription fees plus transaction fees from payroll processing. (Source: search snippet summarising TechCabal/Techpoint seed coverage, https://techcabal.com/2025/06/23/paidhr-raises-seed-round/, checked 2026-11-07; TechCabal itself returned 403)
- Partners with licensed financial institutions for transactions, does not hold customer deposits, earns transaction fees. (Source: https://techpoint.africa/2024/10/21/paidhr-to-enable-employees-receive-salaries-in-49-local-currencies/, checked 2026-11-07)
- Cross-border payroll was 15% of revenue (as of June 2025). (Source: https://techpoint.africa/news/paidhr-seed-funding/, checked 2026-11-07)
- TechCabal ran an analysis headlined "PaidHR is not a fintech" (April 12, 2025). Snippet says the wallet processed "₦1.3 billion ($835,134) in transaction volume monthly since inception", mostly airtime, data, power bills and transport. (Source: search snippet, https://techcabal.com/2025/04/12/paidhr-is-not-a-fintech/, checked 2026-11-07; page 403)
