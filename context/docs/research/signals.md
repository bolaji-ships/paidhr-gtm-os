## Part B: Signals

### Signal 1: Crossing headcount thresholds that trigger statutory obligations

What it is: a company's headcount crosses a level at which a new statutory scheme becomes mandatory.

Why it creates need (sourced):
- Pension: PenCom FAQ (May 2020): CPS covers "private sector organizations with 3 or more employees"; employers with "a minimum of 3 employees are required to obtain a unique identifier called "employer code" from PenCom". https://www.pencom.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/FREQUENTLY-ASK-QUESTION-2020-ENGLISH-MAIN-VISUAL.pdf
- ITF: PaidHR blog: "The ITF levy applies to employers with five or more employees or an annual turnover of ₦50 million or more." https://www.paidhr.com/blog/payroll-compliance (primary Act text not verified; original Act says twenty-five or more employees per search summary of https://www.lawsofnigeria.placng.org/view2.php?sn=205)
- NSITF: PaidHR blog: "All employers with one or more employees are required to register with NSITF" https://www.paidhr.com/blog/payroll-compliance
- PAYE: applies from the first employee (**INFERRED** from employer duty to deduct; DLA Piper: "The employer bears the statutory responsibility for properly deducting PAYE" https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/nigeria/insights/2026/FAQs-on-payroll-taxes-and-employer-liability)
- PaidHR's own complexity thresholds: 10 and 15+ employees (Part A).

Where observable:
- LinkedIn company page employee counts and growth (**INFERRED**).
- Job posting volume per company on LinkedIn Jobs, Jobberman, MyJobMag, Profolio (**INFERRED**).
- CAC filings do not publicly list headcount: Not found.

Recent examples: Not found (no public source tying a specific company to crossing 3/5/15 employees).

Why it creates need: PaidHR: "As headcount grows, the volume of calculations, deductions, and remittances becomes difficult to manage accurately" (https://www.paidhr.com/blog/payroll-compliance). Hiring also triggers onboarding pain: "onboarding quickly turns into chasing documents" (https://www.paidhr.com/blog/types-of-hr-software).

### Signal 7: Audits, penalties and enforcement news
