- **INFERRED**: Two audiences addressed side by side: employer (control, compliance, retention) and employee (money access, wellbeing).
- **INFERRED**: Positioning is shifting upmarket: newer copy says "African enterprises", "enterprise scale", "#1 People Operations Platform", while older pages and about page say "growing businesses".
- **INFERRED**: Inconsistent capitalisation and some grammar slips ("workflows that helps you", "takes times", "value that matter", "talents"), suggesting copy written at different times by different people.

- **INFERRED**: Some stale copy remains (About: "3yrs", "100+ businesses", "12k+ employees paid" vs homepage 400+/570+ businesses, 25,000+ employees).
