### 1.4 Employer obligations under the new regime

Source: DLA Piper (above) unless stated.

- "The employer bears the statutory responsibility for properly deducting PAYE"
- "the tax authority can pursue the employer or manager for arrears where it failed to deduct correctly"
- "Employers must calculate PAYE tax on gratuity before paying it out to exiting employees."
- "Employers are to add bonuses and 13th-month pay to the PAYE base of the employee"
- Foreign-currency pay: "using the CBN official exchange rate at the time the income is paid"
- Deductible items: "Pension, health insurance, life insurance premiums, mortgage interest, and charitable donations"
- Benefits in kind: "These are taxed with a valuation capped at 5% of the asset's cost."
- Annual return: employer is "responsible to file the employee's annual return by 31 January for the preceding year"
- NHF: "Private sector employers are to discontinue deductions for National Housing Fund (NHF) contribution" (see section 3)

Monthly remittance deadline (sources conflict):

## 3. Nigeria: Pension, NHF, NSITF, ITF

### 3.1 Pension Reform Act 2014 (PenCom)

Source: PenCom, "Frequently Asked Questions and Answers on the Contributory Pension Scheme in Nigeria", 3rd Edition, May 2020, https://www.pencom.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/FREQUENTLY-ASK-QUESTION-2020-ENGLISH-MAIN-VISUAL.pdf (checked 2026-11-07)

- Coverage: "The CPS covers employees in the public service of the Federation, Federal Capital Territory, States, Local Governments and private sector organizations with 3 or more employees."
- Voluntary below threshold: "employees of organizations with less than 3 employees as well as self-employed persons can voluntarily participate in the CPS under the Micro Pension Plan (MPP)."
- Rates: "The minimum rate of contribution is 18% of the employee's monthly emoluments where 10% is contributed by the employer and 8% is contributed by the employee."
- Employer code: "All employers that have a minimum of 3 employees are required to obtain a unique identifier called "employer code" from PenCom"; requirements include "Evidence of Taxpayer's Identification Number (TIN)."
- Remittance deadline: "The employer is obliged by law to deduct and remit pension contributions into the employee's RSA not later than 7 working days from the date salaries are paid."
- Penalty: "PenCom would mandate such employer to make the remittance already due, in addition to paying penalty, which would be at least 2% of the total unpaid contributions."
- Monthly emoluments floor: "should not be less than the total sum of basic salary, housing and transport allowances."
- Casual staff: "Employers are mandated to remit pension contribution of every worker on its payroll."

Conflict: PwC Tax Summaries reportedly says employers with at least 15 employees must participate (search summary) https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nigeria/individual/other-taxes. PenCom's own FAQ says 3 or more. Prefer PenCom.
