# PaidHR Buyer Personas

Source tags as in `icp.md` (co, cu, cv, cmp, mk, sg, vo). "Own words" = a customer's words where one exists; where only PaidHR's words exist, that is labelled. Customer quotes from videos are corrected auto-captions: [brackets] mark edits (cv). INFERRED = our reading.

## 1. Champion: HR lead

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Job titles | HR operations specialist (unconfirmed title), HR & admin manager, HR manager, Head of People, HR business partner | cv Video 2; cu §4; sg Part C |
| Measured on | Statutory deductions remitted with evidence; request turnaround (leave, loans, salary advances); fewer payslip requests (INFERRED from their quotes) | cv Video 2; cu §4 |
| Starts looking when | Tax office calls about remittances; a pension complaint or audit letter; headcount passes 10 to 15; new tax law changes calculations | cv Video 2; sg Part A; mk §1 |

Top three pains (own words):
1. "The major HR problem or challenge I faced... [was] remitting statutory deductions... you have to wait for two weeks or more... you keep getting calls from the tax office" (Heroshe, cv Video 2)
2. "most HR software... [is] unable to help most HR professionals process [statutory] deductions" (Heroshe, cv Video 2)
3. "every single time... [employees] always reach out to me and I have to do the manual process" (Heroshe, cv Video 2, on payslips)

What they value (own words): "The turnaround in addressing leave requests, loans, salary advances and expense reimbursement has been very impressive" (Nikky Taurus HR & admin manager, cu §4).

Objections:
| Objection | Basis | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "Will the employee app work?" | Play reviews cite login loops (Apr to Jul 2025), no developer replies | cu §5a |
| "We will only use a fraction of it" | CEO: clients "only unlocked 20-30% of the capabilities" | co §15d |
| "Migration will disrupt payday" | PaidHR blog: "You cannot migrate chaos and expect clarity." | sg Part A |

## 2. Economic buyer: Founder / CEO / MD

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Job titles | CEO and co-founder, founder, managing director (in firms without a payroll team) | cv Video 1 |
| Measured on | Not found. INFERRED: cost, compliance exposure, owner time | open question |
| Starts looking when | "As [the team grew], we realized there was a need for a more sustainable HR solution" | cv Video 1 |

Top three pains (own words, Famasi CEO, cv Video 1 and cu §4):
1. "[Before] using [Pade], everything was manual... It was stressful."
2. "I had to [earmark] about one hour of the day whenever I [had] to run payroll just to manually make payments."
3. "I had to manually make remittances, follow up to make sure taxes and pensions were remitted, make payroll manually and check with the team if they have received their salaries" (written version, cu §4)

What they chose on: "I checked out a couple of them but it just didn't seem very simple to use, and then I stumbled on [Pade] and it looked very simple" (cv Video 1).

Objections:
| Objection | Basis | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "Excel and free tools are fine" | CEO: "a long way to go before startups like his can displace manual processes or free tools like Excel" | sg Part A |
| "What does it cost?" | No published prices; competitors publish (SoftSUITE N3,000 / N5,000 per employee) or go free (Salario) | co §3; cmp §5, §8 |
| "Can I trust a payroll vendor with remittances?" | Bento Africa suspended amid allegations of unremitted tax and pension (INFERRED as buyer worry) | cmp §3 |
