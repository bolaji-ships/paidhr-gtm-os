# PaidHR Messaging

Source tags as in `context/icp/icp.md`. Numbers are PaidHR's own claims unless noted; use only those listed in `facts.md`.

## Core message

One compliant HR platform for the whole employee lifecycle: payroll and remittances done on time with proof, HR work off your desk, and financial wellbeing for every employee at no cost to the business.

Basis: H1 "One compliant HR Software for the entire employee lifecycle." (vo §1); "Control for the business. Wellbeing for the people." (vo §1); EWA "Zero cost to your company" (co §3).
