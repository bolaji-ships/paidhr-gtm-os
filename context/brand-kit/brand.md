| Trait | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|

## Product names
PaidHiring, PaidGuide (AI agent), PaidRight (salary report), PaidLife (employee financial benefits), Cross Border by PaidHR, The Circle by PaidHR, Balanced Scorecard, Leave V2 (vo §4, §11, §13).

## CTAs in use
"Book a Demo", "Get Started", "Talk to a Product Specialist", "Take a Tour", "Contact Sales", "Refer your Employer" (vo §6).

## House rules for new copy
- No em dashes (CLAUDE.md). PaidHR's own LinkedIn About uses one (vo §13); do not copy it.
- Fix known slips when reusing: "workflows that helps you", "takes times", "value that matter", "talents" (vo §12).
