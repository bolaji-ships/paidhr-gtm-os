## Why it works
- **Internal tension as the hook.** The opener is a real behind-the-scenes conflict ("brand team put their foot down"). Readers get a peek inside a well-known GTM brand and want to see what got rejected.
- **Self-deprecating, not polished.** It leans into "too weird and unpolished" instead of hiding it. That makes the company feel human and invites people to take sides in the comments.
- **The caption ends on a colon.** One sentence sets up the video, and the payoff is the video itself, so people have to watch it.
- **Humor drives reactions.** About a third of reactions are "funny" (30 of 95), and the comment-to-reaction ratio is high (38 to 95), which suggests people joined in rather than just liking it.

## How PaidHR could adapt it
- Share a real "the team said no" moment (rejected swag, a scrapped ad, an odd feature request) with the raw artifact attached.
- Keep the caption to one setup line and let the photo or video deliver the punchline.
- Post from a person's profile, not the company page.
