**Target audience (given):** Growing businesses with HQ or payroll in Nigeria (Kenya, South Africa, Ghana secondary), 20 to 200 employees, often with deskless or shift staff (icp.md Layer 1; co §3, §4, §15d).

**Core value proposition:** One compliant HR platform: payroll and statutory remittances done on time with proof, HR requests off the HR desk, and financial wellbeing for every employee at no cost to the business (fx Variant A; messaging.md).

**Key differentiators:** Nigerian statutory depth (PAYE, pension, NHF, NSITF; Innovation Village "Very High" compliance, cmp); remittance receipts as evidence (cv Video 2); EWA at "Zero cost to your company" (co §3); WhatsApp and USSD access for deskless staff, live Oct 2026 in NG, GH, KE (co §15d). The combination is the differentiator, not any single feature (positioning.md notes).

**Patterns identified:**
- **Industries:** logistics, retail and manufacturing are the stated core (cu §2); fintech and VC-backed startups dominate the named logos (cu §2; cv).
- **Sizes:** "mostly under 200 employees" (co §4); both full stories are under 25 people on LinkedIn (cv).
- **Who bought:** the founder/CEO at small firms (Famasi), the HR lead once one exists (Heroshe, Nikky Taurus) (personas.md).
- **Common problem:** statutory remittance (PAYE, pension) done by hand and chased for proof, then payslip and request load on HR (cv Videos 1, 2).

**Targeting recommendation (challenging the given ICP):**
1. **Go below 20 employees for VC-backed startups.** The plan floor is 20 (co §3), but both video customers are around 15 to 22 on LinkedIn and PaidHR says "most startups switch when they reach 10 to 15 employees" (sg Part A). S1 in the signal matrix already starts at 10. Recommend outbound to VC-backed firms from 10 staff.
2. **Treat the 200+ tier as a separate, later motion.** PaidHR has enterprise logos (Oando, Flutterwave, TSL) but none is cleared for copy, and SeamlessHR leads there (fx; cmp §1). Hold until PaidHR names an enterprise reference.
3. **Add a channel audience.** PaidHR's pricing page runs a payroll-bureau offer ("Launch your own Payroll business today!", co §2). Accountants and HR consultants who run payroll for many small clients are an untouched audience (see Creative non-ICP campaigns).

**Best customers for lookalike campaigns:** Famasi and Heroshe (nameable in copy); Busha, Risevest, Bamboo for S1 list seeding; TSL and MAX for S2 list seeding (seeds only, never named) (SM S1, S2).

## No-AI Campaigns

**Payday Is a Calendar Block** (founder, S1 and S3, 10 to 49 staff)
- **Why it works without AI:** tight list (founder title, small team) and the pain is universal at that size. The founder's own words do the work.
- **Core message:** 3 lines. "Most founders at {{company}}'s size block an hour every payday to pay salaries and chase tax and pension remittances. Famasi's CEO did too, until she moved payroll to PaidHR. Worth 15 minutes to take it off your calendar?" (cv Video 1; Famasi named per fx).

**31 January, Plain** (all segments, Dec to Jan)
- **Why it works without AI:** the date is the personalisation. Everyone files.
- **Core message:** "Annual PAYE returns are due 31 January, the first under the new tax law. PaidHR keeps PAYE and pension records in one place, updated for the 2026 law. Want to see the year-end report?" (mk §0, §1.4).

## Front-End Offer Suggestions

1. **PaidRight 2026 Salary Benchmark Report and calculator** (report.paidhr.com, co §2). Already built, useful to every HR lead, and naturally tied to the roles they're hiring for (#5, #6, #7). Low-friction first touch that earns a second email.
2. **Year-end PAYE readiness check (15 minutes, Dec to Jan).** A short call where PaidHR shows what the 2026 law changed for their pay structure (rent relief, bonuses, gratuity; mk §1.3, §1.4) and what the 31 January file needs. Gives value before a demo and fits #2 and #3. Needs PaidHR to agree on the format.
3. **"Run one payroll on PaidHR" pilot.** Let a prospect run one month's payroll and remittance and get the receipts (cv Video 2): the moment the product proves itself. Trial terms are not published (co §3), so this needs PaidHR's sign-off. For Lagos SMEs, the LSETF free 12 months (co §3, §12) may serve the same role if still open.

Note: competitors push free offers (Salario free, Bujeti 2 free months; cmp §7, §8). Don't use price or discount framing (fx banned); lead with the compliance record instead (competitors.md "How to respond").
